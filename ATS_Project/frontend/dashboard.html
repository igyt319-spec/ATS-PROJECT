🛡️ Academic Risk Standing Criteria

Institutional standing is determined through transparent academic compliance thresholds:

🟢 LOW RISK (Safe Track)
Attendance ≥ 85% and CGPA ≥ 7.00, or Attendance ≥ 75% and CGPA ≥ 6.00 with no active risk interventions.
🟡 MODERATE RISK (Monitor Closely)
Attendance between 75.00% and 84.99% OR CGPA between 6.00 and 6.99. Faculty mentor proactive advising recommended.
🔴 HIGH RISK (Critical Action)
Attendance < 75% (violates university exam eligibility) OR CGPA < 6.00. Immediate mentor conference required.
