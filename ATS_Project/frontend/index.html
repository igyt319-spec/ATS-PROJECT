Dynamic Analytics & Digital Twin

Real-time visualizations including Subject Performance Bar Charts, Semester SGPA Trajectory, Attendance Trend with 75% Risk threshold, and OLS regression forecasting.

Intelligent Monitoring Agent

Autonomous heuristic monitor that scans student profiles for declining marks, attendance deficits, and co-curricular gaps with prioritized HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW alerts.
