14 Comprehensive System Modules

Engineered specifically for academic excellence and college viva demonstrations.

🤖 AI Academic Assistant

An intelligent chatbot that analyzes student marks, weak subjects, and attendance to provide personalized study timetables and GPA improvement plans.

📊 Dynamic Chart.js Analytics

Real-time visualizations including Subject Performance Bar Charts, Semester SGPA Trajectory, Attendance Trend with 75% Risk threshold, and Achievement Distribution.

🔍 Intelligent Monitoring Agent

Autonomous heuristic monitor that scans student profiles for declining marks, attendance deficits, and co-curricular gaps with prioritized HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW alerts.

🏆 Achievement Portfolio

Structured tracking for Hackathons, Paper Presentations, Industry Certifications, Technical Workshops, and Sports with verification badges.

🏛️ Faculty & Admin Portal

Complete student management suite with live search, department filters, CRUD operations on marks & attendance, and institutional risk matrix tables.

🔐 Multi-Role Secure Auth

Role-protected routes, JWT token session management, bcrypt password hashing, and zero frontend credentials exposure.
